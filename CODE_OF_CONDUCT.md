# Code of conduct

Mosaikit is a professional project. Everyone taking part — in issues, merge requests,
reviews and any other channel — is expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent;
- criticize ideas and code, never people;
- accept that decisions are documented and can be revisited with new evidence;
- keep discussions on topic and free of harassment or discriminatory language.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to the maintainer (see `publiccode.yml`).
The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
